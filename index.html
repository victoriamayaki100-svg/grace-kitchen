Frequently Asked Questions


  

    

      Do you deliver?
      
Yes, we deliver in Kerry and the surrounding areas, depending on distance. Message us your location and we will confirm if we can deliver to you. You can also collect your order.

    

    

      What are your opening hours?
      
We are open Monday to Sunday, 5am to 10pm.

    

    

      How do I place an order?
      
Go to the Place Your Order section on this page. Choose your food and tap Send Order on WhatsApp. Or tap the WhatsApp button in the Contact section to chat with us.

    

    

      Can I choose my meat?
      
Yes. You can choose beef, turkey, chicken, chicken wings, chicken legs, fish, shaki (tripe), pomo (cow skin) or egg. Choose it in the order form, or call or message us.

    

    

      How do I pay?
      
You can pay by cash, bank transfer or Revolut. We will give you the payment details when we confirm your order on WhatsApp.

    

    

      I have a food allergy. What should I do?
      
Please tell us before you order. All our food is made in the same kitchen, so any dish may contain traces of fish, crustaceans, gluten, eggs and milk.

    

    

      Can I order a cake?
      
Yes. Send us a picture of the cake you want on WhatsApp, and tell us the size and the date you need it. We will agree the price with you.
