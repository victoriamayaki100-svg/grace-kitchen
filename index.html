Frequently Asked Questions


  

    

      How much does the food cost?
      
Small items like swallow, moi moi, chin chin and pepper gizzard have the prices shown on this page. For soups, stew, rice and porridge, there is no fixed price. The price depends on how much assorted you want inside, such as prawns, pomo and different meats, and it can be negotiated with us on WhatsApp. Food prices also change with the cost of ingredients, so we confirm the price with you before you pay.

    

    

      How is the food sold?
      
Rice, yam porridge and plantain are sold by the cooler (half cooler or full cooler). Soups and stew are sold by the pot (medium pot or large pot). Swallow, such as pounded yam, amala, semo and eba, is sold by the wrap. Pepper gizzard is sold by the tray. Meat pies and moi moi are sold one by one. Chin chin is sold in a small take-away pack or a bucket.

    

    

      Do you deliver?
      
Yes, we deliver in Kerry and the surrounding areas, depending on distance. Message us your location and we will confirm if we can deliver to you. You can also collect your order.

    

    

      What are your opening hours?
      
We are open Monday to Sunday, 5am to 10pm.

    

    

      How do I place an order?
      
Go to the Place Your Order section on this page. Choose your food and tap Send Order on WhatsApp. Or tap the WhatsApp button in the Contact section to chat with us.

    

    

      Can I choose my meat?
      
Yes. You can choose beef, turkey, chicken, chicken wings, chicken legs, fish, shaki (tripe), pomo (cow skin), egg or assorted (a mix of all the meats). Choose it in the order form, or call or message us.

    

    

      How do I pay?
      
You can pay by cash, bank transfer or Revolut. We will give you the payment details when we confirm your order on WhatsApp.

    

    

      I have a food allergy. What should I do?
      
Please tell us before you order. All our food is made in the same kitchen, so any dish may contain traces of fish, crustaceans, gluten, eggs and milk.

    

    

      Can I order a cake?
      
Yes. Send us a picture of the cake you want on WhatsApp, and tell us the size and the date you need it. We will agree the price with you.
